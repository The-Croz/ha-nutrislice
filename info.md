# Nutrislice School Menus for Home Assistant

Bring school lunch and breakfast menus from Nutrislice directly into Home Assistant.

### Highlights
- 🔍 **Interactive School Search:** Just enter your district name or paste your school's Nutrislice link.
- 📅 **Native Calendar Entity:** View upcoming breakfasts and lunches on your Home Assistant calendar.
- 🔄 **Calendar Sync:** Optionally copy meals into a Local, Google, or CalDAV calendar you already use.
- 🍽️ **Smart Sensors:** Get today's and tomorrow's main entrees, sides, and nutrition info.
- 🔔 **Easy Automations:** Set up morning or evening notifications without complex Jinja templates.
- 🔄 **Full Backward Compatibility:** Exposes the raw `days` attribute matching custom REST sensors.
